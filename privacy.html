Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 31, 2026

Clutch Drop ("the game") is designed so your data stays on your device. We do not collect, store or sell any personal data, and we do not operate any servers. This policy covers the game on iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple TV and Apple Vision Pro.

Your progress

Your levels, stars, coins, unlocked cars, settings and SPACE RACE best scores are saved only on your device. They are deleted when you delete the game.

No accounts, ads or tracking

The game has no sign-up, no advertising, no in-app purchases and no analytics or tracking SDKs. We do not track you across apps or websites.

Game Center

Leaderboards and achievements use Apple Game Center. When you're signed in, your scores and achievements are shared with Apple and shown to other players under your Game Center nickname, under Apple's privacy terms. We never receive your Apple Account or contact details. You can sign out of Game Center in your device's Settings at any time.

Sharing

When you choose to share a result card, the image and text go only to the app you pick in the system share sheet. Nothing is shared unless you choose Share.

Game engine

The game is built with Unity. The Unity engine may send anonymous technical information about the device (such as model, OS version and graphics hardware) to Unity Technologies to help improve the engine. This information does not identify you. See Unity's privacy policy.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
