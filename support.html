Clutch Drop is a one-tap stacking game: swing the crane, drop the car and build the tallest tower of cars you can, from the street all the way to space.

How to play

  • iPhone, iPad and Apple Vision Pro: tap (or pinch) anywhere to release the car. Mac: click or press Space. Apple TV: press the Siri Remote's touch surface or select button, or A on a game controller.
  • Land the car centred on the one below for a PERFECT drop. Chain perfects for combos; 8 in a row in SPACE RACE triggers OVERDRIVE.
  • Overhangs make the tower lean. Land your next cars on the high side to straighten it before it topples.
  • Wide vehicles like buses and trucks make a stable base; small cars are harder to land.

SPACE RACE

  • Stack in waves of 8 cars. Every 2 waves you reach a pit stop and pick 1 of 3 perks.
  • The higher you climb, the bigger your altitude multiplier: Skyline, Cloud Deck, Stratosphere, Orbit, Deep Space and the Mothership.
  • You start with 3 hard hats. A miss or a collapse costs one; when they're gone, the run ends.
  • The Daily Launch gives every player the same cars and twists each day.

Common questions

Will I lose my progress if I delete the game? Yes. Progress is stored only on your device.

Does it work offline? Yes, the whole game plays offline. Game Center leaderboards update when you're back online.

Can I turn off sound, music or vibration? Yes, in Settings from the main menu or the pause menu.

Leaderboards aren't showing. Make sure you're signed in to Game Center in your device's Settings.
